# Privacy Policy

<p class="stamp">Last updated 24 August 2026</p>

> <span class="hl">Teardown collects nothing.</span> There is no account, no
> server, and no analytics. Everything you save stays on your device.

## What the app stores

When you save a reference, Teardown keeps it on your device:

- the screenshot image you chose to save;
- the two tags you write on it — the product it came from, and the idea it
  might help;
- for a reference shared from a web page, the page's address, its title, and a
  stripped-down copy of its markup.

That is the whole record. The app does not read your photo library, your
contacts, your location, your calendar, or your other apps. It receives only the
individual items you hand it through the share sheet or the add button.

## Where it is stored

On iPhone, images are written to the app's own private data directory and the
records to the app's local database. In a web browser they are stored in that
browser's local storage for the site. The share extension and the app exchange
items through a private App Group container shared only between them.

## What leaves your device

**Nothing, by default.** Teardown has no backend. It does not transmit your
references, your tags, or any usage data anywhere. There is no crash reporting,
no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party analytics SDK.

Data leaves only when you explicitly export it — for example copying a
reference's handoff text, or saving its Figma layers file. At that point it goes
wherever you send it, and this policy no longer governs it.

## Saved web pages do not phone home

When Teardown keeps a copy of a page, it removes scripts, external stylesheets,
embedded frames and refresh directives at the moment of capture. Opening a saved
reference later therefore does not contact the site it came from, and cannot run
that site's code.

## Backups

Because your library lives inside the app, it is included in whatever device
backup you already use — iCloud Backup or an encrypted computer backup. Those
backups are handled by Apple under Apple's terms, not by us, and we cannot read
them.

## Deleting your data

Delete a single reference from its detail screen. To remove everything, delete
the app — that erases its container, including every image, tag and saved page.
Nothing survives elsewhere, because nothing was ever sent elsewhere.

## Children

Teardown is a tool for designers and developers and is not directed at children.
It collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the previous
wording stays in this page's public revision history.
